# ASR Word Error Rate on Indian English

Off-the-shelf models miss drug names and place names hardest. A 300-term biasing list cut WER on clinic intake from 14.2% to 8.9%.

## Why it matters here

This is the assumption the current design rests on. It is worth re-checking.
